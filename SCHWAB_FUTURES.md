# SPX futures CVD

The SPX index has no executed share volume. During cash regular hours, a configured Schwab feed can request ES and MES together with its existing market-data credentials. Market Overview → Market internals → Cumulative volume delta shows the active series, sampled signed volume and coverage. Movement Intelligence, the shared Trade Plan and qualified notification payload receive the same shadow reading. The desk continues to qualify trades using its existing tested inputs.

## What the provider supplies

The read-only acquisition path is the Market Data Production quotes endpoint, with symbols /ES and /MES and the quote and reference field groups. The worker warms these quotes between full scans. The existing in-process cache limits this endpoint to a combined request every three seconds while active. A display running in a different process may poll independently. The adapter requires a real-time FUTURE, a concrete active quarterly contract, recent exchange trade time, last size, cumulative volume and last price. Missing entitlements, delayed quotes, closed sessions or inadequate sampling leave the estimate unavailable.

Schwab also documents Level One Futures streaming. Public client documentation describes last size, total volume, last price, bid/ask and trade/quote clock fields. A Level One update can coalesce many executions and carries no guaranteed per-trade aggressor flag. This release implements the batched quote acquisition path. It does not claim a complete futures tape or a tested Schwab streaming entitlement. Actual availability for this brokerage account requires authorized market-hour observation. [Schwab Market Data Production](https://developer.schwab.com/products/trader-api--individual/details/specifications/Market%20Data%20Production) · [Schwab client streaming fields](https://schwab-py.readthedocs.io/en/latest/streaming.html)

## How the estimate works

- Sample the latest trade size once when its exchange timestamp and the cumulative-volume counter advance. Sign it using the previous uncrossed bid/ask, both observed before the trade and at most five seconds old. When that test is inconclusive, use a change from the previous sampled trade price. Leave an unchanged ambiguous price unclassified.
- Conflation hides intervening trades. Only the last captured size contributes signed contracts. The denominator for coverage is the change in total contract volume across observed within-minute intervals. Gaps crossing a minute boundary cannot be allocated. Coverage describes those measured intervals, not the fraction of the entire exchange tape.
- Require at least three classified samples, two percent classified coverage, observations within twelve seconds of each end of the minute, fresh exchange timestamps and a matching completed SPX minute. These research thresholds are engineering defaults. Gaps, contract changes, active-session changes, counter resets and missing entitlement reset the affected leg. ES can warm up independently of MES.
- The ES and MES pressure values divide each leg's classified signed volume by its own classified volume. Combine the two ratios equally for the research baseline only when contract months and closed minutes match. Add the per-minute ratios across contiguous matched observations for composite CVD. ES is the primary reading; MES gives a second view. Per-leg signed contracts, velocity, acceleration, recent direction persistence, sampled price/flow divergence, and possible absorption/reversal remain separately labeled. This is a heuristic description of measured data.
- The $50 ES and $5 MES multipliers enter each leg's separate classified notional diagnostic. A raw contract sum would give the micro contract the wrong economic weight. CME's customary lead month can change before the nearest contract expires. Use the actual provider-reported active contract, and reset continuity at a roll. [CME contract multipliers](https://www.cmegroup.com/markets/equities/sp-500-and-nasdaq-100-futures.html) · [CME roll dates](https://www.cmegroup.com/trading/equity-index/rolldates.html)
- Quote-based trade classification has known timestamp and inside-spread ambiguities. This implementation uses explicit side inference and exposes coverage. It cannot reconstruct unobserved aggressor flow. [Lee and Ready, Inferring Trade Direction from Intraday Data](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1991.tb02683.x)

## Validation

With live V3 recording enabled, the daily research report compares the same eligible SPX decision timestamps and subsequent price-barrier outcomes for ES, MES, composite and the existing Webull footprint method. It separately counts availability, common origins, directional calls and correct calls. Duplicate option scopes should be evaluated separately in research; overlapping scans do not represent independent trades. The measurements begin with detector build 3.0-shadow.3. A fresh installation has no validated weights, accuracy or economic edge. The observed Webull footprint continues to be the separate input for existing trade qualification and paper fills.
